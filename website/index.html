Price alerts

Not just a notification — a checklist

Every alert shows the drop amount, the new fare, and which of your cruise line's policies applies — Best Price Guarantee, Early Saver, 24-Hour Upgrade Window, and more, depending on the line.

  • Step-by-step claim instructions, written in plain English
  • One-tap call button for the exact department to reach
  • Mark alerts claimed to keep your list tidy
Policies, decoded

Every line's fine print, made readable

Browse the full price-protection policy for any covered cruise line any time — even before you have an active alert — so you know your rights before final payment ever comes due.

On-device AI

A refund assistant that already knows your cruise

Tap "Ask the assistant" straight from a price alert, and it opens already knowing the ship, the sail date, the drop amount, and the exact cruise line policy that applies — no explaining required.

It runs fully on your phone using a locally downloaded LiteRT / Qwen model, sized to your device's RAM automatically. Nothing about your cruises or your questions is ever sent anywhere.
