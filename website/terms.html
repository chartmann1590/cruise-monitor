2. What the Service does

CruiseWatch tracks the publicly posted fare for a cruise you tell it you've already booked, and notifies you when that public fare drops below what you recorded paying. Where available, it shows a summary of the relevant cruise line's own publicly stated price-drop, refund, onboard-credit, or upgrade policy, along with suggested next steps (such as a phone number or claim script) for contacting that cruise line yourself.

CruiseWatch does not book cruises, does not process payments, does not file claims on your behalf, and does not guarantee any refund, credit, or upgrade. Whether you receive anything back is decided solely by your cruise line under its own policy, which can change at any time without notice to us. Always confirm current terms directly with your cruise line before relying on anything shown in the app.
