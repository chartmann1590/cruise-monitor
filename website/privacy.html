Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026 · Terms of Service

CruiseWatch is a free Android app (with an optional Wear OS companion) that tracks the public fare on a cruise you've already booked and alerts you when it drops. This page explains, in full, what data the app collects, why, who it's shared with, and how to have it deleted.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the CruiseWatch Android app (com.cruisewatch.app), the CruiseWatch Wear OS companion app (com.cruisewatch.app.wear), and this website. CruiseWatch is developed and operated independently and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Royal Caribbean, Celebrity Cruises, Carnival, Princess Cruises, or Norwegian Cruise Line.

2. Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address and account identifier, when you sign in with email/password or Google Sign-In (via Firebase Authentication).

Cruise and alert data you provide or that the app generates

  • Cruise details you enter: cruise line, ship, sail date, cabin category, fare paid, currency, and final payment date.
  • Price-check history and price-drop alerts the app generates for your tracked cruises.
  • Whether you've marked an alert as claimed.

This data is stored in Firebase Firestore under your account and is not visible to other CruiseWatch users.

Diagnostics — Crashlytics & Performance Monitoring

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring to catch bugs and slow spots. When the app crashes or hangs, or during normal use, these send Google:

  • Crash stack traces, and the app/OS state at the time of a crash.
  • Device model, OS version, app version, and free memory/storage at the time of a report.
  • App start time, screen render time, and network request timing for the app's own network calls.
  • A random installation identifier (not your name or email) used to group reports from the same device.

This data is used solely to find and fix bugs and performance problems — never for advertising.

Advertising — AdMob

The phone app shows banner and interstitial ads via Google AdMob to keep CruiseWatch free. AdMob may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID (resettable in Android Settings) and general device information.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address, used for ad relevance and fraud prevention.
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks).

You can limit ad personalization at any time in Android Settings → Google → Ads. The Wear OS companion app does not show ads.

On-device app translation — Google ML Kit

You can choose your own language for the app's interface during setup or any time from Settings, from a list of over 50 languages. Choosing a language other than English downloads a small language file from Google's ML Kit Translate service (a one-time download per language, by default only over Wi-Fi). After that download, the app's own interface text is translated entirely on your device — no screen text, cruise data, or account information is sent to Google or anyone else to perform this translation. The app checks your network connection type (Wi-Fi vs. cellular) only to decide whether to ask before using cellular data for that one-time download; this check does not report your location or browsing activity to us or to Google.

On-device AI refund assistant

The optional AI chat assistant runs a language model entirely on your device after a one-time model download. Your questions, the assistant's answers, and the cruise/alert data it references are processed locally and are never sent to CruiseWatch, Google, or any third party. The only network activity involved is the initial model file download from the public model host.

Sharing an alert or cruise

When you tap the share icon on an alert or tracked cruise, CruiseWatch hands the message text to Android's own share sheet — the same system dialog any app uses to share to Messages, email, or another app you pick. CruiseWatch does not see, log, or store who you shared with or through which app; that exchange happens entirely between your device and the app you choose.

4. Data retention and deletion

Your tracked cruises, price history, and alerts are kept until you delete them in the app or delete your account. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, see our dedicated Delete Your Account page for the exact steps, what gets deleted, and what (if anything) is retained and for how long. Requests are processed within 30 days.

5. Your choices

  • Delete individual tracked cruises or alerts any time from within the app.
  • Turn off ad personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Disable notifications for CruiseWatch in your device's app notification settings.
  • Request full account and data deletion any time via our Delete Your Account page.

6. Security

Your cruise and alert data is protected by Firestore security rules that restrict access to your own account only — no other CruiseWatch user can read or write your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Children's privacy

CruiseWatch is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
